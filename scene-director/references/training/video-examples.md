## video-examples-003 — Your First Video — Visual Companion / p.8

Источник: Your First Video — Visual Companion.pdf, стр. 8. Метка: PROMPT / SETUP.

```text
A duel of scales inside a burning nebula that behaves like a cloudscape: banks of dusty rose and salmon with plum shadows, translucent sea-green veils, and a molten red giant filling the eastern sky, its surface crawling with live convection cells. Everything moves. Cloud streams past the lens, hulls sweep past the camera and pull away into fog, and distance is read by what grows and what dissolves in the haze. Two craft only. The wedge: a thirty-meter matte-black faceted attack craft, a flat arrowhead with four round engine bells clustered across its stern, burning white-blue. The leviathan: a four-kilometer charcoal capital ship shaped like a manta ray - one smooth arched dorsal ridge, tapering wingtips, dense plated hull - with a single ventral weapon trench at midship that glows ember-orange whenever the weapon charges, the one breach in its armor. One pilot flies the wedge: a Black man, dark flight suit, full helmet with the clear visor raised, face fully readable. Geography: the duel axis runs west to east; the wedge attacks from the west, the leviathan hangs far east, bow toward the wedge, silhouetted against the red giant. Cameras stay south of the axis, so the wedge reads screen-left flying screen-right into depth and the leviathan holds the deep right of frame; the one exception is Shot 5, flown behind the missile low off its left side. Every exterior camera is itself flying, and the wedge always moves relative to it - passing it, pulling away from it, coming at it, or being chased - so speed reads through cloud tearing past the lens and hull size changing inside the shot. The gap starts at 30 km and the wedge closes to 12 km by the launch. Motion cadence: natural 180-degree shutter, fast passes and streaming cloud carry directional motion blur, holds resolve sharp.
```

## video-examples-004 — Your First Video — Visual Companion / p.9

Источник: Your First Video — Visual Companion.pdf, стр. 9. Метка: PROMPT / SHOT 01.

```text
Shot 1 · 0.0-3.0s · wide at 84°, flying camera moving east a step slower than the wedge, low on the south side of its path, so the wedge overtakes and pulls away; the leviathan lands in the upper-right third. First frame: the wedge's matte-black underside is already sweeping in across the upper-left of frame from behind the camera, meters overhead, cloud shredding off its edges. It passes - the four engine bells flare huge across the frame, their light shafts raking the haze - and pulls away east, shrinking fast, its plumes stretching into four hard points as cloud layers whip past the lens and the far layers between the two craft thin away; the leviathan stands revealed beyond it in the upper-right, a hazed black manta silhouette a tenth of the frame wide against the molten star. Its ventral trench flares ember-orange, vents peeling open - then it fires: a thick crimson energy lance rips west with a tearing bass roar, plants into the cloud deck ahead of the wedge and starts sweeping back toward it, boiling cloud into glowing steam along its path. Hard cut.
```

## video-examples-005 — Your First Video — Visual Companion / p.10

Источник: Your First Video — Visual Companion.pdf, стр. 10. Метка: PROMPT / SHOT 02.

```text
Shot 2 · 3.0-5.5s · wide at 84°, flying camera ahead of the wedge's path on the south side, looking back west at it, then whipping right and down to follow the pass. First frame: the wedge is coming straight at the camera out of the depth, small and growing fast, its bells four blazing points; the crimson lance enters over the camera's right shoulder from behind, a hard line running into the depth toward it, sweeping. The wedge answers: all four bells vector as one, plumes bending off the hull's axis, and it snaps into a hard right roll and dives under the beam - the lance slashes over its dorsal facets within a few meters, crimson glare flooding the hull, heat-shimmer bending the air - as it fills the frame and blasts past the camera's right side close enough for its buffet to shake the frame, vapor ripping off both trailing edges; the camera whips right and down after it and lands on its four bells shrinking into the cloud bank below as it bottoms out of the dive under real mass, punching a wake through the cloud tops. Hard cut.
```

## video-examples-006 — Your First Video — Visual Companion / p.11

Источник: Your First Video — Visual Companion.pdf, стр. 11. Метка: PROMPT / SHOT 03.

```text
Shot 3 · 5.5-9.0s · cockpit interior, handheld at 63°, camera at the pilot's right shoulder, slightly behind and above, looking forward-left across his helmet at the front canopy; his face in three-quarter in the left third, his gloved right hand on the stick in the lower-right foreground. First frame: the wedge is inside the cloud bank - the canopy full of rose-grey fog streaming past the glass in fast ribbons, the horizon gone, the last crimson glare draining off the canopy struts, diffuse cloud light flat on the right side of his face and teal and amber panel light from below on his eye and jaw, a hard catchlight in the eye; the handheld frame still jolting from the pull-out. His mouth is open on a ragged gasp, eyes wide on the fog ahead, his right hand trembling on the stick; the frame steadies as the wedge levels inside the cloud. He swallows. Then the fog ahead flushes ember-orange from within - the leviathan's trench charging for a second burn beyond the cloud - his eyes narrow on that glow, the breath goes long and slow, his jaw sets, the tremor in his hand dies into a tightening grip while the camera creeps a hand's width in toward his eye; his thumb flicks the trigger guard, his index finger squeezes in one clean pull, and the belly bay unlatches with a dull thud that jolts the frame. Then he hauls the stick back into his gut: the nose pitches up hard, the pull pins him down into the seat, the handheld frame sinks with it, and the fog tears open across the top of the canopy into open rose sky as the shot ends. Hard cut.
```

## video-examples-008 — Your First Video — Visual Companion / p.13

Источник: Your First Video — Visual Companion.pdf, стр. 13. Метка: PROMPT / SHOT 05.

```text
Shot 5 · 11.0-15.0s · chase camera at 84°, flying east twenty meters behind the missile and low off its left side, the missile in the upper-right third, its exhaust trail cutting a diagonal from the lower-left corner; the one camera off the south side. First frame: the missile's white-orange motor glare in the upper-right third, its exhaust trail slashing diagonally back past the lower-left of the lens, cloud shreds whipping by, and ahead the leviathan's belly swelling from a dark wall into the whole upper frame, plate after plate racing overhead, the ember-orange trench growing from a spark into an open furnace mouth in the upper right; the missile jinks twice on terminal guidance and the frame lags each jink. The camera arrests short of the plating as the missile buries itself inside the trench - one dark half-beat, the seam's glow choked out - then the belly blows open toward the camera: chain explosions rip along the seams toward bow and wingtips, sheared armor plates tumble past the lens trailing fire, and the shock hurls the camera backward and down so the whole four-kilometer hull falls into view as the frame drops away from it. The detonations race the full length of the ship, the dorsal ridge splits along its spine in a line of fire, both wings fold upward as the spine breaks, and the manta comes apart into three burning masses; then the core flash - a white-orange sphere swelling out of the midship, its light swallowing the red giant and every cloud bank, one shockwave ring racing outward across the entire cloud deck - and the falling hull sections burn down through the deck in a storm of embers as the bloom holds to the final frame.
```

## video-examples-010 — Your First Video — Visual Companion / p.14

Источник: Your First Video — Visual Companion.pdf, стр. 14. Метка: PROMPT / CONSTRAINTS.

```text
POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS Craft in the world: exactly two - the wedge and the leviathan. Crew visible: one pilot, in Shot 3 only. Missiles fired: one. Beam discharges that fire in the clip: one, across Shots 1-2. Hard cuts: exactly four, at 3.0s, 5.5s, 9.0s and 11.0s. The wedge's closest approach to the leviathan across the whole clip is twelve kilometers; the missile alone crosses the final gap. Every explosion originates at the trench. Audio: diegetic only - engines, weapon roar, cloud rush, impacts as they occur on screen; inside the cockpit the pilot's breath sits forward over a muffled engine hum; no music, no score, no bgm, no subtitles.
```
